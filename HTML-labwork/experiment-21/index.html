Demonstration of Array, String and Numeric Functions

Interactive testing studio executing and evaluating PHP's built-in array manipulation algorithms, string formatting and pattern operations, and mathematical precision functions.

functions_demo.php (Complete Implementation) 
<?php
/**
 * Experiment 21: Demonstration of Array, String, and Numeric Functions in PHP
 * Course: Advanced Web Technology (CSIT248) - Amity University Noida
 */

// 1. Array Functions
$fruits = ["Apple", "Banana", "Cherry", "Mango", "Banana"];
$numbers = [45, 12, 89, 34, 67, 23];

echo "Count: " . count($fruits) . "\n";
echo "Unique: " . implode(", ", array_unique($fruits)) . "\n";
echo "Search: " . (in_array("Cherry", $fruits) ? "Found" : "Not Found") . "\n";
echo "Reversed: " . implode(", ", array_reverse($numbers)) . "\n";

// Sorting
sort($numbers);
echo "Sorted Asc: " . implode(", ", $numbers) . "\n";
rsort($numbers);
echo "Sorted Desc: " . implode(", ", $numbers) . "\n";

// 2. String Functions
$str = "Advanced Web Technology in PHP";
echo "Length: " . strlen($str) . "\n";
echo "Word Count: " . str_word_count($str) . "\n";
echo "Uppercase: " . strtoupper($str) . "\n";
echo "Lowercase: " . strtolower($str) . "\n";
echo "Position of PHP: " . strpos($str, "PHP") . "\n";
echo "Replaced: " . str_replace("PHP", "Modern Web", $str) . "\n";
echo "Substring (0, 8): " . substr($str, 0, 8) . "\n";
echo "Reversed: " . strrev("Amity") . "\n";

// 3. Numeric / Math Functions
$val = -42.85;
echo "Absolute: " . abs($val) . "\n";
echo "Round (1 dec): " . round($val, 1) . "\n";
echo "Ceil: " . ceil($val) . "\n";
echo "Floor: " . floor($val) . "\n";
echo "Square Root: " . sqrt(144) . "\n";
echo "Power 2^8: " . pow(2, 8) . "\n";
echo "Min: " . min(15, 82, 4, 99) . " | Max: " . max(15, 82, 4, 99) . "\n";
echo "Random (100-999): " . rand(100, 999) . "\n";
echo "Formatted: " . number_format(1250000.75, 2) . "\n";
?>

Experiment 21: Academic Lab Manual

1. Aim

To design and execute a comprehensive PHP script demonstrating the syntax, operational semantics, and practical usage of key built-in functions spanning indexed/associative arrays, string manipulation, and mathematical/numeric calculations.

2. Core Function Taxonomy

Array Functions

  • count(): Counts array items
  • sort() & rsort(): Asc/Desc sorting
  • in_array(): Value existence search
  • array_unique(): Deduplication
  • array_reverse(): Order inversion
  • array_merge(): Combining arrays

String Functions

  • strlen(): Character length
  • str_word_count(): Total words
  • strtoupper() & strtolower()
  • strpos(): Substring position finder
  • str_replace(): Search and replace
  • substr(): Extraction of substring

Numeric / Math

  • abs(): Absolute non-negative value
  • round(), ceil(), floor()
  • sqrt() & pow(): Exponent/root
  • min() & max(): Bounds detection
  • rand(): Pseudorandom integer
  • number_format(): Currency/comma notation
